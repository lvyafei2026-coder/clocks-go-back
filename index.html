Why Do the Clocks Go Back?

Two simple ideas explain the whole ritual.

☀️ More morning light in winter

Turning clocks back one hour shifts daylight to the morning — darker evenings, but brighter starts to the day. In the US it happens the first Sunday of November; in the UK and Europe, the last Sunday of October.

Fall back = gain 1 hour of sleep 😴

⏰ Easy way to remember

"Spring forward, fall back." In spring you lose an hour; in autumn you gain one. Most phones and computers update automatically — the change mainly matters for ovens, car clocks, and older alarm clocks.

Spring forward = lose 1 hour of sleep ☕
